Where does your line go?

Tap the courses you've finished. The map shows which path you're closest to, the careers it leads to, and what to take next.

How to use it

  1. Check off your classes. Tap every COMD course you've finished. Start with the AAS foundation, then your 3000-level classes. You can pick up to 3 strategy and 4 skills courses, the same as your degree.
  2. See where your line goes. The panel shows the path you're closest to and the jobs it leads to. The arrows on each class tell you whether it leads to a growing, flat or shrinking job.
  3. Explore jobs. After 2 strategy and 2 skills courses, the career planner opens. It ranks jobs by how well your classes fit, shows what employers ask for, and which classes teach it.
  4. Plan your next classes. Pick a job to see which classes to take with your remaining slots. Thinking of switching? "Changing direction" shows what you can still reach without extra courses.
  5. Save your plan. Download a PDF at the bottom and bring it to advising. This is a planning tool, not a degree audit, so always confirm with a COMD advisor and DegreeWorks.
