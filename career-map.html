Strategy and skills courses

You take 3 strategy courses and 4 skills courses, from any line. Stay on your own line, or borrow from another one. The dots show which concentrations list each course. Tap a course to outline the jobs that use it.

3000-level courses open once you finish COMD 2400. Faded courses haven't been offered recently, so the map never recommends them.

Where these numbers come from

About the pay figures

Posted pay comes from real NYC-area job listings found in October 2026. Robert Half figures come from its Salary Guide for New York, NY, where "low" means new to that role, usually with a year or two of work behind them. First jobs out of school often start below it. The whole field line is the national BLS median for everyone in that job, at every level, the same number the Path Finder uses.
